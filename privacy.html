What the contact form collects, and why

If you use the contact form we receive:

  • your name,
  • your email address,
  • your phone number, only if you choose to give it, and
  • the message you write.

We use these only to reply to you about Cureto.

How your message reaches us

Messages are delivered to us through a form service called FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). Your message passes through FormSubmit on its way to our email inbox, and FormSubmit may show you a short check to confirm you are a person. Their own privacy terms apply to that step.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from other companies. The only thing it remembers is your Light or Dark choice, kept in your own browser, and never sent to us.

How long we keep your message

We keep a message until the enquiry is closed, and at most 12 months.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to support@cureto.app.

The law

We handle your details in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
